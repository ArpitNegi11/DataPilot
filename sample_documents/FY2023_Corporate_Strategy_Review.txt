# FY2023 Corporate Strategy & Performance Review
Author: Executive Strategic Planning Committee
Scope: Enterprise Operations & Sales Performance

1. Strategic Objectives
Our target for FY2023 was to achieve a minimum gross profit margin of 22% across all retail and enterprise product tiers.
We targeted rapid expansion in the Consumer segment, seeking to establish market leadership while maintaining strict cost control in regional distribution centers.

2. Regional Guidelines & Expansion
The North and East sales territories were designated as primary growth drivers with dedicated marketing budgets.
Territories experiencing lower penetration (Central and South) are slated for channel partner optimization and localized pricing incentives.

3. Inventory & Product Lifecycle Strategy
Top-tier monitor lines and hardware peripherals remain high-margin priority products.
Product management is mandated to decommission SKUs generating consistent negative returns or margins below 10%, reallocating working capital to high-velocity lines.
