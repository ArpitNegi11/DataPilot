# SaaS Enterprise Revenue Strategy & Retention Memo 2024
Document Reference: MEMO-2024-SAAS-REV-02
Author: Chief Commercial Officer (CCO) & VP of Customer Success
Date: January 2024

1. Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) & Tier Expansion Goals:
- Our primary corporate objective is driving high-margin Enterprise expansion across North America and EMEA.
- Target average Enterprise ARR is established at $150,000+ per client, with minimum committed seats exceeding 100 licenses.
- FinTech and Cybersecurity are designated high-priority strategic growth sectors where customized deployment packages are supported.

2. Churn Risk & NPS Mitigation Protocols:
- Any account exhibiting a Churn Risk Score greater than 60% or NPS score below 6 must be flagged for immediate Executive Sponsor intervention within 48 hours.
- Accounts with Overdue payment status must receive automated payment plan restructuring to protect net revenue retention (NRR).
- Discretionary contract discounts above 20% on Enterprise renewals must be reviewed quarterly to eliminate margin dilution.
