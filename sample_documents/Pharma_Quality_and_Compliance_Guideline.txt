# Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Quality & Audit Guideline
Document Reference: SOP-2023-QC-PHARMA-101
Authority: Quality Assurance (QA) & Regulatory Affairs Board
Effective Date: March 2023

1. Defect Rate Tolerance & Quarantine Protocols:
- Maximum allowable defect tolerance for commercial release is 2.50%.
- Any production batch exhibiting a defect rate strictly exceeding 3.00% must be immediately transitioned into "Quality Flagged" status.
- Quality Flagged batches are strictly prohibited from wholesale commercial distribution and must undergo secondary chromatographic validation.

2. Supplier Quality Standards & Therapeutic Class Oversight:
- Oncology manufacturing involves cytotoxic active pharmaceutical ingredients and operates under zero-contamination tolerance.
- High-volume therapeutic classes (Cardiology, Anti-Infective) stored in Central Hub (Chicago) and Newark must maintain 99.0% on-time audit release schedules.
- Any supplier with repeated batch defect rates above 3% across consecutive manufacturing cycles shall face immediate vendor probation.
